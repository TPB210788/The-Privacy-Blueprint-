Privacy by design

Your family's information isn't the product.

TomorrowKit is being designed from the beginning around data minimisation, meaningful control and privacy by default.

01

Minimum data

We only want the information needed to make the service work.

02

You choose what to share

Forward a message, upload a document or connect services you choose. Whole-inbox access should never be the default.

03

No advertising business model

Your child's schedule should not become advertising data.

04

Control

See what's stored, manage connected services and delete your information.

TomorrowKit is currently in development. Our privacy and security design will continue to be assessed before beta release.

Questions parents ask us

Is TomorrowKit available yet?

TomorrowKit is currently in development. The beta waitlist is helping us decide what to build first.

Will TomorrowKit be free?

We plan to offer a genuinely useful free version. Beta access will be free.

Will I have to give TomorrowKit access to my entire inbox?

No. Privacy and data minimisation are central to the product. We are designing ways for parents to choose exactly what information they share.

Will it work with Google Calendar?

Calendar integration is planned, including Google Calendar. Apple and Outlook support are also being explored.

Is this only for school?

The first version is focused on school, nursery and children's activities because that is where parents tell us the mental load is greatest.

Do you sell family information to advertisers?

No. TomorrowKit is not being designed around selling family information or behavioural advertising.
