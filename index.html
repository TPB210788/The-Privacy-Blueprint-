Questions, answered plainly.

Is TomorrowKit available yet?

TomorrowKit is currently in development. The beta waitlist is helping us decide what to build first.

Will TomorrowKit be free?

We plan to offer a genuinely useful free version. Beta access will be free.

Will I have to give TomorrowKit access to my entire inbox?

No. Privacy and data minimisation are central to the product. We are designing ways for parents to choose exactly what information they share.

Will it work with Google Calendar?

Google Calendar integration is planned. Apple Calendar and Outlook support are also being explored.

Is TomorrowKit only for school?

The first version is focused on school, nursery and children's activities.

Do you sell family information to advertisers?

No. TomorrowKit is not being designed around selling family information or behavioural advertising.
