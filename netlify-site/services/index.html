02

DPIA and AI Governance

Data Protection Impact Assessments completed in line with ICO guidance, plus AI governance reviews for businesses developing or buying AI tools.

What is a DPIA? A structured way to spot and reduce the privacy risks of a new project, system or product. Under UK GDPR it's legally required for some high-risk processing. Getting it wrong means a project is non-compliant before it starts.

What's included

  • Scoping session to decide whether a full DPIA or a documented threshold assessment is needed
  • Full DPIA completed to ICO standard where required
  • AI governance review: data flows, model inputs, supplier agreements and ICO guidance alignment
  • Written output ready to share with stakeholders, regulators or clients

Who it's for

  • Businesses launching new products, features or data-heavy projects
  • Founders and SaaS companies building or integrating AI tools
  • Teams who've tried a DPIA internally but aren't confident it meets ICO requirements
