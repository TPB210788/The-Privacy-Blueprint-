02

DPIA and AI Governance

Data Protection Impact Assessments completed in line with ICO guidance. AI governance reviews for businesses developing or procuring AI tools.

What is a DPIA?

A Data Protection Impact Assessment is a structured process for identifying and reducing the privacy risks of a new project, system or product. Under UK GDPR, a DPIA is legally required for certain types of high-risk processing. Getting it wrong means a project is non-compliant before it starts.

What is included
  • Scoping session to determine whether a full DPIA is required, or whether a documented threshold assessment is sufficient
  • Full DPIA completion to ICO standard where required
  • AI governance review and documentation where applicable: data flows, model inputs, third-party processor agreements and ICO guidance alignment
  • Written output ready to share with stakeholders, regulators or clients
Who it is for
  • Businesses launching new products, features or data-heavy projects
  • Founders and SaaS companies building or integrating AI tools
  • Organisations required by a client or regulator to provide a completed DPIA
  • Teams who have attempted a DPIA internally but are not confident it meets ICO requirements
