Built on in-house experience

The Privacy Blueprint is run by an in-house privacy practitioner with ten years of experience at a major UK charity. That means real operational depth: building and running an organisation-wide DPIA process, managing data subject access requests at volume, and reporting privacy metrics directly to the C-suite.

That experience is built into a proprietary gap assessment tool covering 16 compliance domains, with risk-weighted scoring. It is updated for the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.

This is not a framework applied from the outside. It is a practitioner's approach applied to your business.
