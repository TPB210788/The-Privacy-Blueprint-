Cookie policy

We don't track you

This website does not use analytics, advertising or social media tracking cookies.

What this site stores

We store one small item in your browser's local storage, called tpb-cookie-notice. It only remembers that you've closed the cookie notice, so we don't show it again. It contains no personal information and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

No third party requests

Fonts and images on this site are hosted on our own server, so loading a page doesn't send your details to font or image providers.

Links to other services

Some links take you to other services: our free Health Check, our booking calendar (Google Calendar), our shop (Beacons) and LinkedIn. Those services have their own privacy and cookie policies, which apply once you're on their sites.

Changes and contact

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent first. Questions? Email hello@theprivacyblueprint.co.uk.
