The approach

Most privacy consultants come from legal or advisory backgrounds. This is different.

The Privacy Blueprint is built on ten years of in-house experience running a privacy function inside a major UK charity, solving real compliance problems at real operational scale.

That means the work is grounded in what actually happens inside an organisation: the DPIA request that arrives at the wrong time, the subject access request that lands on a Friday afternoon, the supplier with no data processing agreement, the reporting line that needs educating rather than just a policy document.

The philosophy

Privacy compliance should be enabling, not obstructing

A well run privacy function helps organisations build trust, move faster on products and data initiatives, and avoid the regulatory and reputational cost of getting it wrong.

The Privacy Blueprint exists to make that kind of compliance accessible to founders, SMEs and growing teams who can't justify a full-time privacy hire but need the real thing, not a checkbox exercise.
