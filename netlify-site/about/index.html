About The Privacy Blueprint

A consultancy built on operational experience, not advisory distance.

The approach

Most privacy consultants come from legal or advisory backgrounds. The Privacy Blueprint is different.

It is built on ten years of in-house experience running a privacy function inside a major UK charity, managing real compliance problems at real operational scale. That means the work is grounded in what actually happens inside an organisation: the DPIA requests that arrive at the wrong time, the data subject access requests that land on a Friday afternoon, the supplier with no data processing agreement in place, the reporting line that needs educating rather than just a policy document.

This is not a framework applied from the outside. It is a practitioner's approach applied to your business.

Practical. Documented. No jargon.

The philosophy

Privacy compliance should be enabling, not obstructing. A well-run privacy function helps organisations build trust, move faster on products and data initiatives, and avoid the regulatory and reputational cost of getting it wrong.

The Privacy Blueprint exists to make that kind of compliance accessible to founders, SMEs and growing teams who cannot justify a full-time privacy hire but need the real thing, not a checkbox exercise.
