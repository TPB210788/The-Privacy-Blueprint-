Privacy by design

Cookie Notice

This site is built deliberately to keep tracking to a minimum. There is no advertising network behind it, no marketing pixel and no behavioural profiling. Here is exactly what that means in practice.

Last updated: 14 September 2026 · Applies to: the TomorrowKit website

Our approach

Most websites load a stack of third-party scripts before you have agreed to anything. We have gone the other way. The TomorrowKit site is designed so that the only technologies running are the ones needed to show you the page, plus aggregated statistics that do not identify you.

We do not use:

  • Google Analytics
  • Meta Pixel
  • TikTok Pixel
  • Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity
  • advertising or retargeting cookies
  • behavioural tracking scripts

An honest caveat. Rather than claiming this site sets no cookies at all, we describe what it is designed to do. Hosting platforms can set their own strictly necessary cookies, and third-party services we add later could introduce more. At the time of writing, the only measurement in use is the hosting platform's own built-in, cookieless page-view counter. We check the deployed site and keep this page updated to match what is actually running.

The three categories

1. Strictly necessary technologies

These are needed to deliver the page you asked for and to keep the site secure and stable. They cannot be switched off and, under UK law, they do not require consent. They may include things set by our hosting platform for load balancing, security or fraud prevention.

2. Cookieless site analytics

We want to know which pages people find useful and whether the site works on their device. We do this with aggregated analytics designed to work without tracking cookies and without building a profile of individual visitors. It tells us things like "the privacy section was read 300 times this week", not "this person read the privacy section".

Because this does not store or read information on your device in the way that requires consent, and because it does not identify you, we do not put a cookie banner in your way for it. If that ever changes, we will ask first.

3. Anything optional we add in future

We may one day want to embed something external, for example a short product video. Embeds like that often set their own cookies and can track viewers.

Our rule for that is simple and we are committing to it here:

Nothing that requires consent will load before you have given it. If we add optional or non-essential technologies, we will show a proper consent choice first, make refusing as easy as accepting, and only load the content once you have said yes.

How to control cookies yourself

Every major browser lets you see, block and delete cookies in its settings, usually under Privacy or Site data. Blocking strictly necessary cookies can stop parts of a site working, but it will not lock you out of reading this one.

The ICO publishes clear guidance on cookies and how to manage them at ico.org.uk.

Questions

If something on this page does not match what you see in your own browser tools, please tell us. Email privacy@tomorrowkit.co.uk and we will check it and correct the page if needed.
