Privacy Notice

This notice explains what happens to information collected through the TomorrowKit website and the free beta waitlist. It is written in plain English, because a privacy notice you cannot read is not much use to anyone.

Last updated: 14 September 2026 · Applies to: the TomorrowKit website and beta waitlist

TomorrowKit is still in development. There is no app to sign into yet, so this notice covers the website and the waitlist only. When the beta opens we will publish a fuller notice covering the app itself, and we will tell waitlist members before anything changes.

Who we are

TomorrowKit is a product being developed by The Privacy Blueprint. We are the data controller for the information described in this notice, which means we decide what is collected and why.

You can reach us at privacy@tomorrowkit.co.uk.

What information we collect

If you join the beta waitlist

  • Your email address. This is the only thing we actually need.
  • How many children you are organising for (1, 2 or 3 or more), if you tell us.
  • What stages they are at (nursery or preschool, primary, secondary, other), if you tell us.
  • What causes you the most school-admin stress, if you choose to write something. This box is optional and free text.

What we deliberately do not ask for

The waitlist form does not ask for, and we do not want:

  • your children's names
  • their dates of birth
  • the name of their school or nursery
  • your home address
  • your phone number

Please avoid putting details like these into the free-text box. If you do include them by accident, tell us and we will remove them.

If you email us

We keep your message and our reply so that we can follow up properly and keep a record of what was asked.

When you simply visit the site

We use privacy-friendly, aggregated site analytics to understand which pages people read and whether the site works on their device. These analytics are designed to work without tracking cookies and without building a profile of you. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity or advertising trackers anywhere on this site. Our Cookie Notice explains this in more detail.

Like any website, the service hosting this page also processes technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the page to you and to keep the site secure.

Why we use it

  • To contact you about the beta. Access, testing rounds, and launch.
  • To decide what to build first. The answers about children, stages and stress points help us prioritise features. We look at these in aggregate.
  • To keep the site working and secure. Basic technical operation and abuse prevention.
  • To answer you when you get in touch.

We will not use your email to market unrelated Privacy Blueprint services, and we do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

Our lawful basis

Under UK GDPR we rely on the following lawful bases.

  • Consent for adding you to the beta waitlist and emailing you about TomorrowKit. You gave it by submitting the form and you can withdraw it at any time by using the unsubscribe link or emailing us. Withdrawing does not affect anything we did before you withdrew.
  • Legitimate interests for running and securing the website, for understanding site usage in aggregate, and for replying to messages you send us. Our interest is in making the product genuinely useful; we have considered this against your interests and kept the data collected to a minimum.
  • Legal obligation where we have to keep or disclose information to meet a legal requirement.

Who we use to provide the site

We use a small number of suppliers to run the site and the waitlist. They process information on our instructions only, under contract.

International transfers

Some of our suppliers may store or process information outside the UK. Where that happens, we check that an appropriate safeguard recognised under UK data protection law is in place, such as UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum. If you would like details of the safeguards used for a particular supplier, ask us and we will tell you.

How long we keep information

  • Waitlist entries: until the beta programme ends, or until you ask us to remove you, whichever comes first. If the project does not go ahead, we will delete the list and tell you we have done so.
  • Free-text answers: we aim to work from summarised, aggregated findings and to delete the original free-text responses once the research round they belong to is finished.
  • Emails you send us: normally up to 24 months after our last exchange.
  • Site analytics: kept in aggregate only, so it is not linked to you as an individual.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • be told how your information is used, which is what this notice is for
  • get a copy of the information we hold about you
  • have mistakes corrected
  • have information deleted in certain circumstances
  • ask us to limit how we use it while a concern is looked into
  • object to us using it where we rely on legitimate interests
  • ask for it in a portable format where the right applies
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we relied on consent

To use any of these, email privacy@tomorrowkit.co.uk. We will respond within one month. There is no charge in normal circumstances.

The quickest version: if you want off the waitlist, reply to any email from us or write to us with "remove me" and we will delete your entry. You do not need to give a reason.

How to contact us

Email privacy@tomorrowkit.co.uk for anything about privacy or your information, or hello@tomorrowkit.co.uk for anything else.

How to complain to the ICO

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator.

  • Website: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
  • Helpline: 0303 123 1113
  • Post: Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF

Changes to this notice

TomorrowKit is in active development, so this notice will change as the product takes shape. We will update the date at the top whenever we change it. If a change materially affects how we use information you have already given us, we will contact waitlist members directly rather than quietly updating the page.
