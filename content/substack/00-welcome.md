## Why I started this

I've spent ten years running the privacy function inside a large UK organisation. Subject access requests, impact assessments for new projects, supplier contracts, data breaches, board reports. The real, day to day work of keeping personal data safe.

Over that time I noticed something. Large organisations have whole teams for this. Founders and small businesses usually have a free policy generator and a vague worry that they're missing something. Often they are, and it only comes to light when a client, an investor or the ICO asks a question they can't answer.

The Privacy Blueprint exists to close that gap.
